Layer 4

Human Governance Gate — Autonomy Level 1

A real, named reviewer resolves every Decision. Nothing commits to the graph of record without this step.

Approve

Writes a real graph node — Control, Finding, Risk, or the full Assurance chain — attributed to the reviewer.

Feeds back into Layer 1 — the next cycle reasons over a strictly larger graph.

Reject

status → REJECTED, with an optional note.

No graph mutation. The loop stops here — cleanly, not silently.

Why it compounds

This is a loop, not a pipeline

Every agent observes with a filter along the lines of NOT (:Decision)-[:ABOUT]->(x) — fetchRequirements, fetchUnassessedSignals, fetchUnscoredFindings, fetchUnbundledEvidenceIncidents all work this way. That means every approval at Layer 4 doesn't just record one outcome — it permanently shrinks next cycle's observe set, and permanently grows the traceability chains in the cross-cutting layer above.

The graph an agent reasons over on cycle N+1 is strictly richer than the one on cycle N: more resolved controls, more scored risks, more bundled evidence, sitting right there as precedent. That's the learning — not weight updates, but a self-narrowing, compounding graph that every future cycle, and every future audit, inherits for free.

Open

The loop above closes within one agent's lane — it doesn't yet close across agents. An approved Risk doesn't currently retrigger control-intelligence to reconsider a Control. Closing that is Scenario Simulation, and it's still unscoped: there's no working definition yet of what a "scenario" is in this domain.
