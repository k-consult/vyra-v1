# 6. Compliance Autonomy Model

## Purpose

Define trust boundaries for autonomous operation.

---

## Level 0

Human Driven

Humans perform all actions.

---

## Level 1

Agent Recommended

Agents provide recommendations.

Humans execute actions.

---

## Level 2

Agent Assisted

Agents prepare actions.

Humans approve execution.

---

## Level 3

Agent Executed

Agents perform approved actions automatically.

Humans oversee outcomes.

---

## Level 4

Fully Autonomous

Agents perform and validate actions independently.

---

## Governance Rule

Higher autonomy requires stronger assurance, verification, and auditability.
