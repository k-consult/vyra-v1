# Vyra Platform Architecture v7

## Architecture Deliverables Plan

### Status

**Blueprint v7: Baselined**

The architecture blueprint has been reviewed, refined, and approved as the canonical architectural foundation for Vyra.

The following concepts are considered frozen:

* Continuous Compliance Loop
* Compliance Engine
* Multi-Agent Compliance System
* Compliance Digital Twin
* Five Enterprise Graphs
* Traceability Intelligence
* Assurance Graph
* Shared Memory Architecture
* Compliance Decision Architecture
* Compliance Autonomy Levels
* Regulatory Trust Architecture

The remaining work focuses on visualization, refinement, narrative generation, and executive communication.

# Success Criteria

The final architecture should position Vyra as:

"An Agentic Risk & Compliance Infrastructure platform that continuously transforms regulations into operational assurance through a network of collaborating AI agents operating on shared enterprise memory."

The architecture should be remembered for three ideas:

1. Continuous Compliance Loop
2. Compliance Digital Twin
3. Traceability Intelligence

These become the defining concepts of the Vyra platform.
